Orpheus Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orpheus Bot ("Orpheus") handles information when you use the bot.

1. Information Orpheus May Receive

When Orpheus is active in a Discord server, Discord may provide the bot with information necessary for its features to function. Depending on the feature being used, this may include:

  • Discord user ID and username.
  • Server and channel information.
  • Messages or commands directed to Orpheus.
  • Information required to operate bot features.

Orpheus is designed to respond to messages when it is specifically mentioned or when a supported command is used.

2. AI Processing

Messages sent to Orpheus for AI responses may be processed by the AI system used by Orpheus.

During development, Orpheus may use a locally running AI model on the developer's computer. If external AI services are added in the future, this policy may be updated to describe them.

3. Storage

Orpheus does not intentionally sell personal information.

Information may be temporarily processed or stored when necessary for bot functionality. Specific storage practices may change as features are developed.

4. Third-Party Services

Orpheus may use third-party services such as Discord, Spotify, or other services for specific features.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Orpheus is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Orpheus develops. The latest version will be made available at the published Privacy Policy address.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Orpheus, contact the bot's developer through the Discord server where Orpheus is being used.
